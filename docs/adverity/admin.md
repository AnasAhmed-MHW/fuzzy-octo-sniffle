# Sharing Access

## Giving Someone Access to Adverity  

You will need `admin` access to share access with an individual.  

1. Click **Administration** on the bottom-left toolbar.  
2. Under the **Other** section, click **Users**.  
3. Click **Create User** on the top-right of the screen.  
4. Fill in the required details and click **Create**.  

---

## Deleting or Deactivating a User  

If you need to remove a user, it is recommended to **deactivate** their account instead of deleting it. Deactivating ensures that there are no issues with assets they have created, authorised, or adjusted within the platform.
