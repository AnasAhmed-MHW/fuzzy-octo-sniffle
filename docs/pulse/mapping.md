# Mapping and Cateogrising the questions

Over time and over the years there have been numerous itterations of the survey itself. 
This is due to changes in client requirement, addition of language, maybe understanding that the question was unclear and improving the language or even an engineering issue on focal data side which caused a change win formatting. 

Therefore there is a need to ensure there a somewhat dynanamic way of mapping various questions (with there little nuances) to a column header in a table. 

To this effect there is are two files that are pertinant `mapping.py` and `questioncat.py`. As this is only available for New Balance right now a sample can be found in the `/new_balance` folder.
